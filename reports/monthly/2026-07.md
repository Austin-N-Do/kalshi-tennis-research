## If we had never exited early (hypothetical)

The table above is the factual result and the basis for everything in
this record. This one is a **counterfactual**: it re-prices the same
positions as though no exit rule had fired and each had ridden to
settlement. No money moved this way and nothing here is a claim of
profit -- it exists to size what the exit rules cost or saved.

**Read this with suspicion.** It is retrospective, computed on the same data that produced it, and it assumes a held position could always have been carried -- ignoring that capital tied up in one position blocks another when the caps bind.
